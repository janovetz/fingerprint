02Trays

Each set is one full drawer of trays.

03Joinery

Target size. Rounded to an odd count, so both wall ends get full fingers at the top and bottom.

How far below the tray rim the dividers stop. Use 0 for flush.

04Material & laser

Use the measured thickness. Nominal 1/4" plywood is often 0.22–0.24".

Use 0 if the cutting service compensates for kerf. Check with them; many do. On your own laser, about 0.15–0.2 mm.

05Sheets

Walls are nested with the grain running lengthwise, as in the photo. Floors run grain along their long side. Floors too big for the stock are cut in pieces. Seams run under a divider when one is in range (hidden and locked by its tabs). Otherwise the pieces join with glued puzzle keys.

06Engraving

Add labels per compartment from the tray inspector. Area burns the letters solid, like a stamp. Line traces their outlines, which is faster and cheaper. Long labels shrink to fit.

07Export

The ZIP includes nested sheets (SVG + DXF), one DXF/SVG per unique part with its quantity in the filename, a CSV cut list, and this project file.
